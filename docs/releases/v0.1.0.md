# Budgey 0.1.0 — first release 🐦

<!-- Paste this into GitHub → Releases → "Draft a new release" (tag v0.1.0). Links are written to work from the release page and assume the default branch is `main`. -->

The first public release of **Budgey**, a friendly, private budget tracker for Android. Track purchases,
budgets and subscriptions, scan receipts, split checks — and keep everything on your phone.

> This is an early release (0.x). It's ready for everyday use, but expect rough edges — please
> [open an issue](../../issues) if something goes wrong. Make an occasional **Settings → Export** backup.

## Download

**`Budgey-0.1.0-release.apk`** (attached below). Open it on your phone and allow installing from that
source when asked. Requires **Android 12 or newer**.

## Highlights

### Purchases
- A big overview chart — donut, pie, bars, trend or rings — with the month's total, the change vs. last
  month and how much budget is left. Tap a slice to drill into a category; **touch and hold** to preview
  one and slide around the chart to preview others (with haptic ticks).
- Add by hand or **scan** a receipt. ~950 merchants, banks and services get their logo automatically.
- Left something half-added? The **⟲ Resume** button next to **+** brings it back exactly as you left it.
- Search, rich filters, and multi-select to categorize or delete in bulk (with undo).

### Scanning
- Reads **receipts, banking-app screenshots, and gas-pump or kiosk screens**: merchant, total, date, and
  whether it's a purchase or a subscription. Fully discounted receipts come out as $0.00.
- Runs entirely on the phone. Optional on-device AI scanners: **Gemini Nano** (on phones that have it) or the
  downloadable **Vision AI** model (phones with 8 GB+ RAM). Your photos never leave the device.
- **View text** shows exactly what the scanner read; **Rescan** / **Advanced Rescan** try again.

### Budgets & categories
- Nested category folders; weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly budgets that roll up into parents.
- See what's on pace, ahead or over at a glance.

### Subscriptions
- Monthly and yearly totals, what's coming up, renewal reminders, and automatic payment logging.
- **Free trials done right**: nothing is charged or shown during the trial, and you're warned (with a
  notification) when it ends — keep it or cancel it.
- Price history (e.g. a student discount that ended) and pause / cancel / resume without losing the past.

### Calendar
- A month view shaded by spending, with purchase counts and renewal marks. Tap a day for its purchases.

### Tools
- **Check splitter** — scan the bill, then split evenly, by custom amounts/percentages, or by item.
  "5 Burgers $50" becomes five burgers you can hand out to different people. Choose who chips in for the tip.
- **Tip calculator** and an **offline currency converter** (~160 currencies built in).

### Yours to tweak
- Light, dark or pure-black themes, budgie or Material You colors, font and text size, an Animations switch.

## Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Everything is stored on your phone. Budgey only goes online when *you*
download an AI scanner model or turn on daily currency-rate updates — and those requests carry no personal
data. **Export / import** everything (receipt photos included) as one `.zip`.

## Good to know

- **Install over a previous build?** If you installed Budgey before from Android Studio, this APK may be
  signed with a different key. Export a backup first, uninstall the old one, install this, then import.
- Renewal reminders need notification permission — Budgey asks on the Subscriptions page.
- Known issues and planned fixes are listed in [docs/AUDIT.md](../../blob/main/docs/AUDIT.md); ideas for what's next are in
  [FUTURE_IDEAS.md](../../blob/main/FUTURE_IDEAS.md).
