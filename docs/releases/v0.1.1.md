# Budgey 0.1.1 — fixes & polish 🐦

<!-- Paste this into GitHub → Releases → "Draft a new release" (tag v0.1.1). Links are written to work from the release page and assume the default branch is `main`. -->

A fix-up release: everything found in a full read-through of the app (October 2026) has been dealt with.
No new features, just fewer surprises. It installs over 0.1.0 and keeps your data.

## Download

**`Budgey-0.1.1-release.apk`** (attached below). Open it on your phone and allow installing from that
source when asked. Requires **Android 12 or newer**.

## What's fixed

### Money & dates
- Amounts with **decimal commas** ("4,50", "1.234,56"), spaces or apostrophes as thousands separators,
  and negatives in parentheses are read correctly.
- Yearly subscription totals no longer drift by a few cents, and short amounts ("$1.3K") round properly.

### Scanning
- Dates follow your phone's **day/month order**, and a "Dec 28" scanned in early January lands in the
  right year. Words like "Decaf" or "Mayo" are no longer read as dates.
- Dashes on a receipt aren't mistaken for minus signs; grouped thousands are read correctly.
- **Refunds and returns** are recognised and saved as refunds.
- In the check splitter, **suggested tips** ("18% tip 3.13") no longer become the tip.
- Tilted photos are straightened before reading, so labels and prices pair up correctly.
- The scan screen has **Cancel** and **Try again** (with the same photo), friendlier error messages,
  and a scan you're reviewing isn't lost if Android closes Budgey in the background.
- The Vision AI model stays loaded while the camera is open, so scans start faster.

### Subscriptions & reminders
- Saving a subscription is all-or-nothing (no half-saved price history).
- "Renews today" reminders aren't skipped when you open the app before 9 AM.
- Payment dates you set by hand are kept.
- The **Every N** cycle field can be cleared and retyped, and earlier price periods can use a custom cycle.
- Budgey asks for notification permission once you add a subscription, says plainly when
  notifications are blocked, and a tap after "Don't allow" opens system settings.

### Purchases
- Bulk **move / delete** only touches the purchases you can see after searching or filtering.
- **Clear all** keeps the category or payment method a list was opened for.
- Custom date ranges read "from Mar 3 to Apr 2"; searching stays smooth with lots of purchases.

### Backups & privacy
- Backups include every setting; damaged rows in a backup are skipped and counted instead of changed.
- **Undo erase** puts everything back exactly as it was. The safety copy can be deleted early.
- No cloud backup. Moving to a new phone brings your data across, but never the post-erase safety copy
  or downloaded AI models.

### Everywhere else
- A purchase or subscription that was deleted opens as "This purchase is gone" instead of a blank screen.
- The trend chart copes with refunds that push the total below zero.
- Easier-to-read budget and refund colours, and "ahead of pace" is said in words, not just colour.
- Better **TalkBack** support: date fields, colour swatches, switch rows and long-press actions.
- Dialogs and sheets keep what you typed when the phone rotates.
- No white flash on launch for dark-theme users; the text-size setting works with Android 14's
  font scaling.
- The right tab stays highlighted, and tapping the current tab takes you to its top.

## Good to know

- Installs over **0.1.0** — your data stays. (If your 0.1.0 came from Android Studio with a different
  signing key: export a backup, uninstall, install this, then import.)
